## 2 · The Product

_Pull this from your adopted pitch, then sharpen it as a team — this is
refinement, not re-ideation._

**The problem:** Freshmen and new students don't know how their campus actually works. Official college websites cover the basics, but the practical knowledge (which entrance to use, where to study, which programs are worth joining) only spreads by word of mouth, so new students miss out.

**Who it's for:** College students, especially freshmen and transfer students who are new to campus. Upperclassmen are the ones who share tips and experience.

**Three core features (the MVP):**

1. **Accounts and profiles:** Students can sign up, log in, and edit a profile with their major, interests, and social links.
2. **Posts feed:** Students can create posts with pictures, and other students can like and comment on them.
3. **Student Guide:** A section where students post tips organized by category or major. Examples include "CTP for CS majors," "take the tunnel when it rains," and "best water fountains."

**What ships by Week 13 (demo day):** A live website where a visitor can create an account, set up a profile, and browse a feed of real posts with photos, likes, and comments. They can also open the Student Guide, filter tips by major or category, and add their own tip. The site will be pre-filled with real posts and tips so it doesn't look empty.

**Out of scope / v2 ideas (Week-9 pitch fodder):**

- Direct messaging between students
- Professor and class reviews
- Campus events calendar and club pages
- An interactive campus map linked to tips
- Supporting multiple colleges, where each student only sees their own school
- Verifying students with a .edu email
- Notifications
- Reporting and moderation tools
